Google Play Data Safety Compliance

Account & Data Deletion

Official account and user data deletion portal for all applications published by theKami (Developer: Kumail Raza Bangash) on the Google Play Store.

Overview & User Rights

In strict accordance with Google Play's User Data - Account Deletion policy, we guarantee users full control over their personal data. If you have created an account or used synchronization features in any app published under the developer entity theKami, you can permanently request the deletion of your account and all associated personal records at any time.

Deletion permanently purges your account authentication, synced cloud profiles, activity logs, and personal identifiers from our cloud databases (Google Firebase).

Supported Applications by theKami

ShapeVibe: Exercise & Yoga

com.thekami.shapevibe Developer: theKami

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion

  1. Open the ShapeVibe app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Navigate to Privacy & Security / Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm the confirmation prompt.

Timeline: Processed immediately.

Method 2: Web / Email Request

  • If you have uninstalled the app or lost device access, email us directly at theKami350@gmail.com.
  • Use Subject: [ShapeVibe] Account & Data Deletion Request.
  • Include your registered email address or account ID.

Timeline: Processed within 48 to 72 hours.

Data handling details for ShapeVibe:
  • Data Permanently Deleted: Firebase Auth user ID, registered email address, cloud fitness progress, workout logs, daily streak history, and profile metadata.
  • Data Retained: None. All user records are completely removed. (Anonymized crash telemetry managed by Google Play / AdMob is subject to standard Google retention).

LuminaWalls: 4K & Live Wallpapers

com.thekami.lumina_walls Developer: theKami

In-App Deletion / Reset

  1. Open LuminaWalls.
  2. Go to Settings > Account / Data Privacy.
  3. Tap Clear Favorites & Account Data.

Email Request

Send an email to theKami350@gmail.com with Subject: [LuminaWalls] Data Deletion Request.

Data handling details for LuminaWalls:
  • Data Deleted: Favorite wallpaper lists, user preferences, and cloud sync records.

Scuba Sweep Adventure

com.thekami.scubasweepadventure Developer: theKami

In-Game Data Reset

  1. Open Scuba Sweep Adventure.
  2. Go to Options / Settings > Reset Game Progress & Data.

Email Request

Send an email to theKami350@gmail.com with Subject: [ScubaSweep] Score & Account Deletion.

Data handling details for Scuba Sweep Adventure:
  • Data Deleted: High scores, game unlocks, and cloud leaderboard profiles.

Submit a Manual Deletion Request

If you prefer manual deletion or have questions regarding your data across any app by theKami, email our support team directly. Requests are processed within 48-72 hours.
